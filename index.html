Scale of Preference Calculator

This free scale of preference calculator helps you decide what to buy first. List your wants and needs, arrange them in order of importance, enter your income, and the app instantly highlights the items you can afford. Everything below the line shows what you will have to give up.

What is a scale of preference?

A scale of preference is a list of a person's wants or needs arranged in order of importance, with the most pressing want at the top. Because resources such as money and time are limited, a scale of preference makes it clear which wants should be satisfied first.

Why is a scale of preference important?

  • It helps individuals, firms and governments make rational use of limited resources.
  • It makes sure the most important wants are satisfied before less important ones.
  • It shows the opportunity cost of a choice: the next best alternative given up.
  • It prevents impulse spending and makes budgeting easier.

How to use the scale of preference calculator

  1. Set your income and currency from the edit button next to Income.
  2. Tap + to add each item you want, with its price.
  3. Use the up and down arrows to arrange items from most to least important.
  4. Highlighted items fit within your income; the rest cannot be bought this time.
  5. Create a separate named budget for each month, or duplicate one to start next month's list.

Scale of preference example

Suppose you have $500 and want a headphone ($150), a laptop bag ($250) and a water bottle ($120), in that order. The headphone and laptop bag cost $400 together, leaving $100, which is not enough for the water bottle. The water bottle is the opportunity cost of your choice.

Frequently asked questions

How is a scale of preference related to opportunity cost?

When your income runs out, the first item on the scale of preference that cannot be bought is the opportunity cost: the next best alternative you give up.

Is this scale of preference calculator free?

Yes. It is free, needs no sign-up, and keeps your data in your own browser.

Can I keep more than one list?

Yes. Create as many named budgets as you need, for example one per month, each with its own items and income.
